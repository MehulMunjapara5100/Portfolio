    Understand your brand, audience, goals, and Shopify requirements to establish a strong foundation.

  2. Plan

    Test and refine performance, responsiveness, usability, and key conversion details.

    Complete final checks, launch your store, and provide support for ongoing improvements.

FAQ

Explore our FAQs

Answers to questions about process, pricing, timelines, and project details

What Shopify services do you offer?

I specialize in custom Shopify storefronts, UI/UX design, Shopify theme development, performance optimization, conversion-focused improvements, third-party integrations, responsive design, and ongoing store support.

Can you build a completely custom Shopify store?

Yes. I can design and develop a Shopify storefront around your brand, products, customers, and business goals. The result is a tailored experience rather than a generic, one-size-fits-all theme.

Can you customize or improve my existing Shopify store?

Absolutely. I can improve an existing Shopify store through design refinements, custom sections, UX improvements, responsive fixes, performance optimization, and new functionality without necessarily rebuilding the entire store.

Do you design Shopify stores from scratch?

Yes. I can handle both the design and development process, from structuring the user experience and creating the visual direction to developing the final Shopify storefront.

Can you improve my Shopify store's conversion rate?

I focus on creating smoother product journeys, clearer navigation, stronger calls to action, and frictionless shopping experiences. While no conversion result can be guaranteed, the store is designed with conversion best practices and your business goals in mind.

What do you need to get started?

Usually, I’ll need information about your brand, goals, Shopify store, products, required functionality, design references, and any existing assets or designs. From there, we can define the scope and next steps.

Can you work on a specific Shopify task instead of a full store?

Yes. You can hire me for focused Shopify work such as a custom section, responsive fixes, UX improvements, performance optimization, integrations, or other specific development requirements.

Do you provide ongoing Shopify support?

Yes. I can continue working with you after launch to refine the storefront, fix issues, add functionality, improve performance, and support your store as your business grows.
